1 Executive Summary & Central Message

Central Thesis Statement
"Modern wealth management is dangerously fragmented across legacy banking apps, physical safe lockers, and unencrypted spreadsheets, while existing commercial SaaS tools leak sensitive balance sheet data to public cloud LLMs. Wealth OS solves this by engineering a 100% private, sovereign financial operating system that unifies multi-asset valuation, AES-256-GCM encrypted estate vaults, and zero-cloud on-device WebGPU artificial intelligence."

Wealth OS is an enterprise-grade, privacy-first personal wealth management and digital succession architecture. Developed as a final-year engineering and business capstone project, Wealth OS achieves three core architectural breakthroughs:

  • Multi-Asset Portfolio Intelligence: Real-time automated valuation and loan amortization for physical real estate, liquid equities, luxury timepieces (Rolex market index tracking), motor vehicles (depreciation modeling), and contingency liquid reserves.
  • On-Device Financial AI Advisory: Browser-native Large Language Model execution via WebGPU & WebLLM, ensuring zero financial telemetry or balance sheet data ever leaves the user's physical machine.
  • Cryptographic Vault & Digital Succession: AES-256-GCM enveloped document storage with automated nominee inheritance protocols and instant Chartered Accountant (CA) tax compliance dossiers.

2 Project Inception & Development Journey

The development of Wealth OS followed an iterative, evidence-based engineering methodology spanning 4 distinct milestones over 16 weeks:

Figure 1: Iterative 16-Week Capstone Engineering Lifecycle

Milestone 1: The Fragmentation Problem (Discovery Phase)

Observation: High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNWIs) and affluent professionals manage assets across 8–12 disconnected tools (demat accounts, banking portals, physical vehicle logs, land title registries, insurance policies, and spreadsheets).

Core Pain Point: When debt is incurred or physical assets depreciate, spreadsheets fail to calculate true Liquid Net Worth, Fixed Obligation to Income Ratios (FOIR), and Estate Succession Gaps.

Milestone 2: Modular Architecture & Mathematical Modeling

Replaced fragile client-only JSON stores with a robust, transactional SQLite relational engine. Built deterministic mathematical algorithms for:

  • Reducing Balance vs. Flat Interest Loan Amortization with exact monthly interest/principal schedule partitioning.
  • Indian Income Tax Act (1961) Section 80C, 24(b), and 80D deduction optimizers with Old vs. New Regime auto-comparison.
  • Actuarial Human Life Value (HLV) income multiplier and mortality risk coverage models.

Milestone 3: Cryptographic Security & Vault Infrastructure

Implemented AES-256-GCM Envelope Encryption with unique per-file Data Encryption Keys (DEKs). Developed an automated digital will engine with claimant authentication, biometric file locks, and HMAC-signed time-locked emergency tokens.

Milestone 4: Zero-Cloud Client-Side AI (WebLLM Integration)

Replaced vulnerable third-party cloud AI APIs with browser-native WebGPU acceleration using MLC-AI WebLLM. Quantized LLMs (Llama-3-8B-Instruct, Gemma-2-2B, Phi-3.5) execute directly on user GPUs with zero remote server data transmission.
